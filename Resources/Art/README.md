# Contemporary encounters

19 works by six artists: Todd Williamson, José de Martín Simón, Fons Heijnsbroek, Ben Vollers, Peter Klashorst and Marike Bok. A mix of luminous abstraction, gestural painting, portraits, surreal scenes and mixed-media constructions. These replace the familiar art-history collection.

Images were retrieved from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-10-18. Each source identifies an artist upload, reviewed artist Flickr release, or Wikimedia permission ticket. The full source revision, rights metadata, download URLs, SHA-256 hashes and display crops are recorded in [catalog.json](catalog.json). Dates are artwork dates where documented; `Undated` means the source supplies no reliable work date. Camera timestamps are not used as artwork dates.

Downloaded JPEG/PNG renditions are bundled unchanged. The app crops, zooms and overlays a contrast gradient when displaying them. Adapted CC BY-SA display versions retain the same CC BY-SA license as their source image. The MIT source-code license does not replace these image licenses. Artist credits and links remain visible in the app; no artist endorsement is implied.
