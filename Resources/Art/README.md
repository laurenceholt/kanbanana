# Art, up close

42 recognizable works from prehistory through the late twentieth century: cave painting, Egyptian and Greek sculpture, Indian bronze, Chinese landscape, Benin ivory, Renaissance and Baroque paintings, Japanese printmaking, Impressionism, early abstraction, modern sculpture and architecture. This is a small survey, not an exhaustive canon.

The bottom of both Focus and the full board identifies the current work, maker and date, with a link to its Wikipedia article or museum page. **Art library** lets you select a work, read image credits, or visit the original image and its reuse terms. The collection continues rotating hourly.

Images were obtained from Wikimedia Commons on **9 October 2026**. Individual file pages identify them as public domain, CC0, or Creative Commons licensed; the exact image license is listed below. The five CC BY / BY-SA photographs retain their photographers' credits and licenses. Modern public sculpture is represented by photographs made in the United Kingdom; the architecture photograph was made in Spain. Image licenses do not transfer ownership of the depicted artworks.

Downloaded JPEG/PNG files are bundled unchanged. The app downsamples display copies into bounded caches, preserves color, and applies a focal crop, zoom and readability overlay. Those display transformations are modifications; CC BY-SA images and their adapted display versions remain under their respective CC BY-SA licenses. No images are downloaded at runtime, and no conversation content is sent to Wikimedia or a museum. Opening a credit link opens the external artwork page. Earlier Photos preferences automatically select this replacement collection.

[Catalog and provenance](catalog.json) records the title, artist, date, medium, article, image source, download URL, SHA-256, crop parameters, and source rights metadata for every asset. Approximate dates describe the work rather than the photograph. No artist, museum or Wikimedia endorsement is implied.
