# Art, up close

24 details from The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access collection. The hourly sequence mixes palettes, places, periods and media: woodcuts, wallpaper, embroidery, oil painting, stone sculpture, porcelain, metalwork, manuscripts, mosaics and stained glass. Focus and wide views use individual focal points and zooms; the picker links to the complete work.

All selected records had `isPublicDomain: true` and an image supplied by the museum API when checked on **9 October 2026**. Images are available under [CC0 through The Met's Open Access program](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access). This selection is based on individual records, not an assumption that every museum image is unrestricted. [The Met Collection API](https://metmuseum.github.io/) supplied the metadata and original image URLs.

Original JPEGs are bundled unchanged. The app downsamples decoded copies into bounded caches, preserves color, and applies a focal crop, zoom and readability overlay during display. No images are downloaded at runtime, and no conversation content is sent to a museum. Earlier Photos preferences automatically select this replacement collection. The picker and **Next artwork** keep the existing hourly rotation behavior.

[Catalog and provenance](catalog.json) records full museum titles, attribution, date, medium, credit line, object ID, original download URL, SHA-256 and crop parameters. Short display labels below sometimes describe the selected detail instead of using the complete museum title. No museum endorsement is implied.
