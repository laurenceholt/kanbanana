# Photos appearance assets

The collection contains **18 photographs**: 12 AI-generated images and six sourced photographs. All are bundled locally and rotate hourly; Appearance → Photos → Photo library selects an individual photograph. The app never downloads or generates backgrounds at runtime. Thumbnail and display copies are downsampled into bounded caches, converted to monochrome, and cropped to fill the window. Original bundled files remain unchanged.

## Sourced photographs

Source and licensing pages were checked on **15 September 2026**. These are individual public-domain/CC0 selections, not a blanket assumption about Wikimedia or other free-photo sites. Five are Wikimedia Commons Featured Pictures; Lightning is featured on several language Wikipedias. The jellyfish was also a Picture of the Year 2019 finalist.
